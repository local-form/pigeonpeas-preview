House favorites

Handcrafted pizzas, signature egg rolls and plates piled with rice and peas, plantain and cabbage. Ask about the vegan version of any favorite.

Oxtail Egg Rolls

Crispy outside, slow-braised oxtail inside. A vegan oxtail version is on the menu too.

Patties

Jamaican Patties

Beef, jerk chicken, salmon and callaloo in a flaky golden crust, plus vegan beef and chicken patties.
